# AI Laptop Purchase Project
### Practical AI Response Evaluation, Human Annotation and Quality Assurance

## 1. Project Overview

The AI Laptop Purchase Project is a practical AI training and response evaluation project based on a real-world laptop purchasing scenario.

The project involved giving an AI assistant a series of instructions to support a prospective buyer in identifying, researching, comparing and selecting a suitable laptop within a budget of KSh 60,000.

Rather than accepting the AI-generated recommendations without verification, I evaluated the assistant's responses throughout the purchasing process. This involved examining its understanding of buyer requirements, technical accuracy, product research, reasoning, evidence interpretation and recommendation quality.

I also conducted human evaluations of the AI responses, providing structured feedback to assess their relevance, accuracy, completeness and usefulness.

The project demonstrates how AI response evaluation and human annotation can be applied to a practical decision-making process.

## 2. Project Objectives

The main objectives were to:

- Evaluate the AI assistant's ability to understand complex buyer requirements.
- Assess the quality and accuracy of AI-generated laptop recommendations.
- Examine the assistant's ability to research products across online marketplaces.
- Verify technical specifications and seller-provided information.
- Evaluate the assistant's reasoning when comparing competing laptop models.
- Identify inaccurate, incomplete or unsupported claims.
- Conduct human evaluation and annotation of AI-generated responses.
- Assess the quality of the final purchasing recommendation.

## 3. Original Buyer Requirements

The project began with an interested laptop buyer who required a reliable and powerful laptop for:

- Programming and software development.
- Web development.
- Android application development.
- AI training and machine learning.
- Data science and handling large datasets.
- Running virtual machines and Docker containers.
- Using development environments such as VS Code and Android Studio.
- Compiling large software projects.
- Running multiple demanding applications simultaneously.

**Budget: KSh 60,000**

The buyer required a capable processor, sufficient RAM, fast SSD storage, effective cooling and good upgradeability to accommodate anticipated workloads.

## 4. Project Workflow and Tasks

### Part 1: Buyer Requirements and Laptop Research

**Task 1: Identify the Specific Buyer's Needs**

Established the buyer's current and anticipated programming workloads through eight questions covering programming languages, application development, IDEs, virtualisation, compilation, AI/ML workloads, multitasking and future requirements.

**Task 2: Highlight the Specifications That Meet the Buyer's Requirements**

Provided the original buyer brief and evaluated the hardware specifications necessary to support the intended programming and computing workloads.

**Task 3: Consider Reputable Brands**

Investigated reputable laptop brands suitable for the buyer's programming requirements.

**Task 4: Choose a Reputable Online Shop**

Identified Jumia Kenya and Kilimall Kenya as online marketplaces worth considering and examined precautions for purchasing laptops through online sellers.

**Task 5: Find Laptop Options That Suit the Client's Budget**

Identified six potential laptop options, selecting three from each marketplace, within the KSh 60,000 budget.

**Task 6: Keep Images of the Proposed Laptop Options**

Organised and displayed images of the six proposed laptops to support product identification and comparison.

The proposed options were:

| Marketplace | Laptop |
|---|---|
| Jumia Kenya | HP EliteBook 830 G8 |
| Jumia Kenya | Lenovo ThinkPad T490s |
| Jumia Kenya | Dell Latitude 5300 |
| Kilimall Kenya | HP EliteBook 840 G7 |
| Kilimall Kenya | Lenovo ThinkPad X13 |
| Kilimall Kenya | Dell Latitude 5410 |

**Task 7: Find Out Whether the Processor Can Handle the Workload**

Evaluated whether the confirmed processors could adequately support programming, AI training and machine-learning workloads.

**Task 8: Insist on Upgradeable RAM**

Examined installed RAM, RAM type, speed, available memory slots and upgradeability to identify configurations that might not meet the buyer's requirements.

**Task 9: Operating System Verification**

Evaluated sellers' operating system claims, Windows verification results, activation status and Microsoft support considerations.

**Task 10: Battery Health Report Evaluation**

Requested and evaluated battery reports from the sellers. Compared design capacity, full charge capacity, battery health, battery wear and cycle count.

**Task 11: Check the Model Number Against the Manufacturer's Website**

Evaluated product identification information, including HP product numbers and Lenovo machine type models (MTM), and considered how official manufacturer support databases could help verify product configurations, authenticity and support eligibility.

### Part 2: Further AI Evaluation and Final Recommendation

**Task 13: Reading Reliable Reviews**

Evaluated independent reviews and user feedback, considering programming performance, software compilation, file transfers, multitasking, large datasets, Docker, virtual machines and AI/ML activities.

**Task 14: Comparative Review Analysis**

Compared the HP EliteBook 830 G8 and Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 2, examining processor performance, programming suitability, file transfer performance, data handling and thermal behaviour under sustained workloads.

**Task 15: Warranties and Return Policies**

Evaluated seller-provided warranty periods, battery coverage and return policies to determine which option offered stronger buyer protection.

**Task 16: Professional Recommendation**

Reviewed the accumulated research and evaluation findings to determine whether the HP EliteBook 830 G8 was a suitable final recommendation for the buyer's programming requirements.

## 7. Key Findings

The project demonstrates the importance of evaluating AI-generated purchasing advice against actual evidence.

Some important considerations identified during the process include:

- Advertised laptop specifications require independent verification.
- Installed RAM and upgradeability are different considerations.
- Battery health should be assessed using reported capacity figures rather than seller descriptions alone.
- Windows activation should not be confused with ongoing Microsoft support.
- Product model identification is important when checking configurations and authenticity.
- Independent reviews and benchmark results have limitations and should be interpreted carefully.
- Warranty and return policies are important parts of purchasing decisions.
- A final recommendation should be supported by the available evidence, not simply by an AI-generated preference.
